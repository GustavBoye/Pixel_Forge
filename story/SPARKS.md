# Sparks

Premises the world already supports, each with the hard part filled in: **the choice**.
Copy one into a new story, answer the other five spark lines, write.

Marked ⛓ where existing lore fixes the ending — those write fast, because you already
know where they land.

---

**1. Urba** ⛓
The one always left to man the wall. Not quite human — nose and ears too big, snow that
doesn't melt on his skin.
*Choice:* he lets something through the gate, or hides what he is to keep the only post
that will have him.
*Lands on:* asleep in the snow at Wolfspine, and a boy he's never met covers him with a fur.

**2. The Sealing** ⛓
The bricking-up of the old trade passage, told from inside the fortress the day it happened.
*Choice:* someone helps wall it, knowing who is still on the other side.
*Lands on:* white bricks, a red claw, and a clinking nobody will explain for generations.

**3. The Plague**
The sickness that took everyone except the Huldus.
*Choice:* a Huldu who knows why their people were spared decides whether to tell anyone.
*Note:* whatever the answer is, it has to also explain why the soldiers say "if the
Bloodguard finds out, they'll hang them."

**4. Uther's Problem**
The human king sets a question of mathematics or natural science. Berrick solves it.
Tell it from the gnome who was given the same question first — and failed.
*Choice:* he can pass the answer to the newcomer, or let him fail as he did.

**5. The Reek**
Clear water, mind parasites, and a beast at the end of it.
*Choice:* someone drinks knowingly — to save a life, to end one, or because the thirst won.
*Note:* the only story here where the protagonist can't narrate the last act. Use that.

**6. The Archivist**
Five thousand years of gnomish records in the deep mountain.
*Choice:* a gnome finds the entry that proves the founding was a lie, and decides whether
to file it, burn it, or copy it out.
*Pairs with:* Berrick's story — this is its long echo.

**7. Winterkiss**
The berry Ulfir meant to pick, and the one he actually did.
*Choice:* a forager who knows the difference sells the wrong basket to the right person.

**8. The Stillborn**
The Vibkriseer is said to have brought a dead baby back once.
*Choice:* hers — what she took from somewhere else to do it.
*Note:* keep the price concrete and small. It's worse that way.

**9. The First Redcloak**
The day the Bloodguard first walked into Hrafvik.
*Choice:* the man who let them in, and what he was promised.
*Lands on:* elders who go inside and shut the door while a boy is robbed in the street.

**10. The Coup** ⛓
The Nisaland political elite take the mountain back by force once Uther's alliance lapses.
*Choice:* a gnome loyal to the old king decides whether to fight for a lie he now knows about.
*Lands on:* the wary, boomstick-pointing hold that jails a human boy carrying a body.

**11. Vidrik's Huldu Kid**
The story the soldier tells at the feast — a huldu child he saw once.
*Choice:* what Vidrik did about it at the time, which is why he's still telling it.
*Note:* this one is a story inside a scene. Budget it short, or write it full and quote a
piece of it in the novel.

---

## Adding to this list

A spark is ready when you can say the **choice** in one sentence and it isn't
"they decide to go." Going is Act 2. The choice is the thing they'd rather nobody found out.
