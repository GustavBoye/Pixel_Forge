# Chapter 10: A Warm Welcome

The days past and when Ulfir had finally recovered from his injury they finally set out to bring Figdal back to his people.

They walked through the old forest until the mountainside came into view. Ulfir thought of wolfspine fortress but he could tell that they were much further east.

Ulfir carried the body and in front of him Culprit led the way. Culprit's little body swaying from side to side as he trekked through rocks and roots. He knew nobody in Hrafvik would ever believe what happened if he told them.

"We are there soon" said Culprit.

they walked and walked alongside the mountain, the came to a place where the mountain itself seemed to have been split, they walk through, and inside was a clearing with a lot of moss growing, and at some point they started walking up alongside the mountain, and sudden in the middle of nowhere, embedded into the mountain itself there was a simple wooden door. it was fairly small as if made for kids. Ulfir felt concerned for the size.

dont worry its bigger inside Culprit the gnome assumered him.

He crunched over and managed to step inside without dropping Figdal.

Dont expect a warm welcome" Culprit said concerned.

They stopped inside and true enough, there was plenty of space to the ceiling at least 4 meters. stairs carved out of the mountain lead deeper into the mountain. and ulfir could see there was more light and openness in the end of the passage.

They get inside and then suddenly a lot of gnomen guards point their gnomen boomsticks at him.

they see that Ulfir is carrying the body of Fignal the Gimbal. "What is this?"

"Redcloaks" Culprit said with a trembling voice, "They killed him, and almost killed me, but the human saved me" Culprit said pointing his small finger at Ulfir.

They guards gave them a suspicious look.

They ask Ulfir to handle over the bolts of the crossbow, but the crossbow itself they let him keep respectfully.

on the way to their jailcell Culprit explains that the doors here a big for trading with Huldus, but no all part of the empire has such human friendly design. its a luck Ulfir is small.
