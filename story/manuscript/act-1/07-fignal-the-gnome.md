# Chapter 7: Fignal the Gnome

On a crisp, clear day when the birds sang in lively harmony, Ulfir ventured deeper into the forest than he had since the Grukk attack on Hrafvik. He was gathering berries, his thoughts drifting aimlessly, when a tiny voice pierced the silence.

"Ouch!"

He stopped abruptly, his eyes scanning the forest floor for the source of the sound. Surely, it must be a mouse, he thought. But mice don't speak. Yet it couldn't possibly be a human.

Curiosity pushed him forward, and he crept toward the crest of a small hill. As he peeked over, his breath caught, and he froze.

Below, two Bloodguards loomed over a small figure, shoving it to the ground. A gnome.

He had almost dreamt of seeing one. But not like this.

Two bloodguards were kicking him around, kicking him to the ground. "Stop it!" Every time the gnome was knocked down, he managed to stand up again. But each time, the guards placed their feet on his shoulders and shoved him back to the ground. "No, ouch, stop it!" the gnome repeated, his voice barely audible.

Anger began to boil inside Ulfir. But this time felt different.

He remembered the time he had returned from the marketplace, exhausted from a full day's work, only to be robbed of his coins by a group of bloodguards.

The worst part was that, at that moment, a group of elder fellow Norinheimers had seen it, but did nothing but rush inside, turning a blind eye. He cursed them, not only the Bloodguard, but Norinheimers too.

Ulfirs thoughts raced, torn between action and inaction.

*If I don't act now, I'm no better* he thought

"Ouch, that is not nice!" the small voice continued.

"Let's throw him in the river," one of the bloodguards laughed.

Ulfir loaded his crossbow, the tension in the string rising like his own fury. But he felt frozen, paralyzed with fear. He ran his tongue over his two missing front teeth, a sharp reminder of the last encounter with the Bloodguard.

"Stop messing around, we have a job to do," One of the guards said, the sound of a sword being drawn following.

But before he got a chance to pierce the gnome with his sword. Ulfir appeared before them.

"Go away!" he yelled, aiming his crossbow at the bloodguards.

One of them glanced at him, and then burst into laughter. "what is this?," he scoffed as he walked towards him.

"Go away now or i will shoot you!" Ulfir repeated, his voice firm.

The bloodguard raised his shield and charged toward him. Before Ulfir could react, a fist struck his chin, knocking him to the ground.

*not again* Ulfir thought. The bloodguard punched him again before pulling him up and sat him on a tree stomp, and Ulfir watched as the other bloodguard took his crossbow. He felt no pain, only shame.

"Thank you!" the bloodguard mocked. "Where did you steal this from? Doesn't matter, thank you, it must be valuable." as he inspected the crossbow. Then the other guard aimed his fist at Ulfir, as if aiming his next strike. He waved it in front of Ulfir's face and with a cracking sound it his his nose. his nose started to feel cold, and dripping.

Ulfir fell back. "Can you please let me go now?" he asked, blood dripping from his mouth, a drop nearly landing on the bloodguard's tunic.

"What would you have done if that drop had landed on my armor?" the bloodguard asked in a threatening tone. Ulfir looked at the newly polished armor and said "I don't know,".

In the corner of his eyes he saw the gnome made it's escape with it's almost comical way of moving.

The Bloodguard lifted the crossbow, now aiming at his heart. Ulfir's mind raced, he did not want to leave this earth yet.

"Wait, what's that?" the other Bloodguard said as he took Ulfir's bag of berries and started eating.

"This tastes amazing. See that's how northern demons find food," he continued.

"It does taste good," he said, but after eating the handful of berries he aimed at Ulfir yet again, staring him down with his small cruel eyes.

"Tell him to take off his clothes first; we can sell it. I don't want blood on it." The Bloodguard reluctantly lowered his aim before saying "Do as he said"

Ulfir felt ashamed, because for a moment he almost did what they said. Instead he prayed to the all-father of a way out. And he promised if to the all-father that if he spared his life. He would bring goddess to this world.

In that moment the eyes of the Bloodguards turned white and they fell to theirs knees and started groaning.

"Quick we must go" the small Gnome was pulling Ulfir hands. "Quick, before they turn" he said.

Ulfir tried to take back the crossbow. But the blinded Bloodguard held on as his skin turned white. The Gnome used both hands to remove the grip of the Bloodguard.

"Quick, they will turn" the Gnome said as they rushed away.

"What do you mean turn?" Ulfir asked as he stumbled along with the Gnome.

"It's the berries. You probably thought you were picking winterkiss didn't you?" The gnome replied. "My mentor is around, he will now how to fix you. He is Gimdal Gnome." his little voice said proudly.

They stumbled around the forest looking for the Gnomes mentor but when they did, they found him with the face in the ground. With a deep cut in his little shoulder.

"Fignal, what happened" the Gnome asked his mentor.

"Redcloaks" Figdal exhaled weakly.

"Please help me carry him". the gnome said " I know where to get help for both of you".

Ulfir managed to pick him up despite his whole body shaking.

"Follow me the" the Gnome said as he pulled Ulfir towards the direction of the dark forest.

They walked through tall trees, dead trees, living trees and trees with white leaves. The whole time Ulfir was wondering how much further he could go before he would drop to his knees, but he managed to continue.

They came to a reek where the water flew. The water seemed clear and Ulfir halted to drink.

"You can't drink that water!" the Gnome said.

"Why not?" Ulfir said about to ignore the Gnomes warning.

"Mind parasites" the Gnome said. "It will turn anyone into a vicious beast"

Ulfir looked down on Figdal the Gnomes mentor. He had a white beard and looked older than this world. But despite that he was laying in Ulfir arms like a sleeping baby with closed eyes. Altough shallow breath and barely clinigng on to life. They crossed the reek, and not before long they reached a clearing. In the clearing stood a house, with a ancient tree growing through it.

"There.." said the Gnome.
