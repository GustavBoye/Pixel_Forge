# Chapter 2: The Passage

The next morning, Ulfir woke early. He grabbed a thick fur to keep warm and draped it over his shoulders as he slid out of bed.

Before the sun could rise, he crept through the great hall, stepping carefully over the drunken soldiers sprawled across the floor.

Once outside, he made his way to the steps of the wall, climbing up to the battlements where the cold air bit at his face. From there, he looked out across the horizon, his gaze sweeping over the land.

The moon hung high in the sky, casting a eerie, cyan color across the snowy expanse. The world below seemed as if enchanted by ancient forbidden magic, even the small bits of snow landing on his lips had a taste of electricity and sorcery.

The silence was absolute, broken only by the whisper of the wind. It felt as though he was the only soul awake in all of Urgård.

Further down the wall, Ulfir saw someone sitting with his back against the wooden timbers. He was asleep next to a small fire burning in a metal drum, the kind used for makeshift warmth. His face was so pale, and bits of snow rested on his skin. Ulfi wondered if he was dead, but to his relief, he could hear a gentle snore. The man's face was strange, as if not quite human; his nose and ears seemed too big, as if his body was older than his soul.

Ulfir wondered if it he had been guarding the wall all night. Was it Urba?.. "poor Urba"?, Ulfir took the fur off his shoulders and placed it over the man to keep him warm.

He continued down the wall, moving toward the ancient section of the fortress. Here the stone seemed to merge seamlessly with the mountain itself.

He carefully descended the black, icy steps, where the moonlight had turned the frost into a shimmering silver carpet.

At the base of the stairs, he came upon a sealed passage, its entrance blocked by a wall of massive white bricks, worn and weathered by the passing of time.

The surface still bore the faint, fading symbol of the Bloodguard: the red claw with a drop of blood, its once-vibrant hue now dulled and cracked.

Ulfir jumped, his heart racing as a jolt of anxiety shot through him. He spun around, only to find the woman from the night before standing beside him. She had appeared so quietly, it was as if the air itself had delivered her. Her skin was impossibly pale, almost translucent, as though the light of the moon itself had taken form.

"This is where they used to trade in ancient times," she said, nodding toward the wall. "Before they sealed it off"

Ulfir hesitated, his eyes still fixed on the sealed passage. "Who?" he asked, nervously.

She smiled softly, as if enjoying his curiosity.

"Come... listen," she said, walking toward the wall and pressing her ear against the cold white stones.

He approached the wall, leaning in to press his ear against the cold stone. And then he heard it.

Clink, clink, clink... It sounded like chains. Or perhaps dozens of small pickaxes chipping away at the mountain.

"What's in there?" Ulfir asked. She smiled softly, then quickly glanced at the first rays of sunlight creeping over the horizon. With a nervous flicker in her eyes, she vanished into the crumbling corridors of the ancient fortress.
