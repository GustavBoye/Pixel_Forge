# Chapter 9: Healing

The following days Ulfir had to recover from his injuries. the days unfolded in slow rhythms, marked by Ulfir's steady recovery under the care of the Vibkriseer and her daughter, Lily.

The mornings began with the chirping of forest birds, the sunlight filtering through the ancient tree that towered above the house. Ulfir awoke to the scent of herbs and potions brewing in the Vibkriseer's cauldron, a routine that became both familiar and comforting. His body ached less each day, though his pride bore wounds far slower to heal.

It was Lily who seemed to bring the most change in him.

At first, their interactions were brief, limited to moments when she offered him water or handed him a poultice for his injuries. Her quiet presence was a stark contrast to the chaos he had endured, yet there was a warmth in her gestures that Ulfir found himself drawn to.
