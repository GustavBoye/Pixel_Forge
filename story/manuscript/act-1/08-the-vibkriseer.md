# Chapter 8: The Vibkriseer

They approached the house, Ulfir's pulse quickening with each step. His mind filled with questions about who could be inside. He knew better than to disturb a Vibkriseer, But he could not carry Fignal any longer, and he desperate needed water before passing out.

The gnome gave a polite knock, using his tiny fist. He shot a nervous glance at Fignal, knowing time was slipping away. When no one responded, he knocked again, more firmly yet still courteous. As the seconds dragged on, panic took hold. He began hammering on the door with both hands, shouting, "Emergency and urgency, please!"

The door opened and a woman came to view. Dressed on black silk, she looked as if she had just woken up. Clearly annoyed by have being woken. But her expression changed when she saw Fignal. They quickly got Figdal inside on a table and she started tending his wound and mixing potions. Ulfir sat on a wooden tree-legged chair. Out of the blue appeared a girl, she was his age. She was offering a wooden cup of water.

"Thank you so much" Ulfir said, as he quickly swallowed all the water. For a brief moment he forgot the situation he was in, as this was water was the best he had ever tasted. He wondered if it was enchanted water. However he was still not feeling great. His whole body was shaking as he watched the Vibkriseer and the Gnome attend to Figdal.

"will he survive" Ulfir asked the girl.

"I believe so. My mother even brought back a dearborn baby once" she replied.

Ulfir asked for more water and noticed the girls appearance. Black eyes, black slik clothing and black messy hair. Her mother was cursing as she spilled various liquids desperately trying to mix the right potion for Figdal. One of the potions fell to the floor, filling the air around it with thick damp.

The Gnome was sitting next to Figdal with a frown, writing in his journal. His eyes turned watery as he put on his gnomen googles. His hand writing was more beautiful than anything Ulfir had ever seen. He did not know what it meant, he only saw the beautiful symbols.

"What do you think he is writing" The girl asked him.

"Exactly my thought." Ulfir said, suspicious if she had read his mind.

"My mom says gnomes document everything" She said.

"Everything worth of noting they document. And in the depth of the mountain they keep it stored, for at least 5000 years they have been documenting" She continued.

Ulfir wanted to know more, but he was not feeling well. He needed to sleep and recover from his conciousion.

After a while the Vibkriseer said "There's nothing more i can do"

"We must bring him back home for a proper funeral" the Gnome said.

They showed Ulfir to a room where he was told to lay down in darkness. They said he could loss his memory if he didn't.
