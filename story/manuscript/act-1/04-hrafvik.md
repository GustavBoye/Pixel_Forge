# Chapter 4: Hrafvik

The coastline emerged from the mist, where dark blue waves hurled themselves against jagged rocks, spraying salt into the cold, biting air.

In the distance, the town of Hrafvik clung to the horizon, its faint lights flickering weakly, as though ready to give up entirely.

As they entered the town, a heavy silence hung over the streets, broken only by the soft sobs of townsfolk. Soldiers stood in a tight circle near the center, their faces grim and pale, murmuring to one another in hushed voices.

And then they saw it.

Lying in the dirt, its body twisted and still. It was a dead Grukk, its dark hide stained with blood and soil.

Snow began to fall, soft and mournful, landing on the blue tunics of the fallen Norinheimers.

Haldor came running, his breath visible in the cold air, his expression tight with worry. "Are you all right?" he asked, his voice tense, searching for any sign of Thorild and the others.

"Father" Thorild called out, urgency in his tone. "We need to send reinforcement's to Wolfspine"

Haldor nodded grimly, his concern shifting to resolve. "We'll send men at dawn," he said, placing a reassuring hand on Thorild's shoulder. "Thank the gods you're safe."
