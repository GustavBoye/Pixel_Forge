# Open questions and continuity flags

Things I noticed reading the draft straight through. Nothing here has been changed in the
manuscript — the prose is preserved as written. Resolve, then fix the text.

## Naming

1. **The mentor gnome has four names.** Chapter 7 is titled *Fignal the Gnome*; inside it
   Culprit says his mentor is "Gimdal Gnome," then calls him "Fignal," and the narration
   calls him "Figdal." Chapter 8 uses both Fignal and Figdal in the same paragraph.
   Chapter 10 calls the body "Fignal the Gimbal." Pick one and sweep.
2. **The young gnome is unnamed until Chapter 10**, where he is suddenly "Culprit" with no
   introduction. Either name him at the rescue or have Ulfir ask.
3. "Culprit" reads as a word, not a name — worth a second look, unless the oddness is the
   point.

## Structure

4. **Chapter 6 is missing.** The draft goes 5 → 7. Either write it or renumber.
5. **Chapters 8–13 are all headed "Chapter X"** in the draft. Numbered here by position.
6. **Chapter 9 (Healing) breaks off mid-scene** after the line about Lily's warmth.
7. **Act labels repeat**: "Act 1: The Ordinairy World" covers Chapters 1–3, then "Act 1:
   Call to Action" covers 4–5, then chapters carry no act label at all. Also *Ordinairy* →
   *Ordinary*.
8. **Chapter 12 leaves Ulfir's POV** for the first time (Ramdal at the fortress). If the
   book is close third on Ulfir throughout, this is a real structural decision, not a
   scene choice.

## Plot and logic

9. **The Haesling rules contradict the joke.** The soldiers say the Haesling story is a
    prank and the woman is only a Huldu — but she flees at the first ray of sunlight in
    Chapter 2, exactly as the "prank" described. Deliberate? If so, make sure the reader
    can feel it's deliberate.
10. **Who sealed the passage, and why is the Bloodguard's mark on it?** The mark is faded
    and cracked, which puts the Bloodguard there long ago — that changes what they are.
11. **What is behind the wall?** Chains or pickaxes. Ulfir never gets an answer, and it
    hasn't paid off yet in any later chapter.
12. **The berries.** Ulfir picked them, carried them, and presumably intended to eat them;
    only the Bloodguards do. Worth one line establishing he never tasted them, or the
    scene invites "why isn't he white-eyed too?"
13. **What does "turn" mean?** Culprit says it twice with real urgency and it is never
    answered.
14. **"He would bring goddess to this world."** Reads as a typo for "goodness," but as a
    vow to a god it may be exactly right. Flagging because the whole promise hangs on it.
15. **The three see nine figures in the mist** and can't tell if they're Grukks — yet in
    Chapter 4 a dead Grukk lies in Hrafvik and nobody connects the two out loud.
16. **Wolfspine's fate is never returned to.** Chapter 3 leaves a battle starting; the
    story then spends six chapters elsewhere. Chapter 12 is the earliest payoff. Consider
    whether Ulfir hears any news at all in between — the silence could be the point, but
    it should be a chosen silence.
17. **Distances are soft.** Wolfspine is a hard day's climb from Hrafvik, but the trio
    make it home the same day they leave, through the old forest.
