## Act 1 — The Ordinary World

### Chapter 1 — Urgård
- [x] Ulfir, Baldur and Thorild climb to Wolfspine Fortress with supplies.
- [x] The southern gate is shut when it is normally open — first wrong note.
- [x] They meet General Ramdal; the crossbows carry the Bloodguard's red claw.
- [x] Ramdal calls a feast; the hall is half-empty.
- [ ] **Ulfir sees the strange troll man** (Urba) — currently he only meets him asleep
      on the wall in Chapter 2. Plant him here first, glimpsed across the courtyard or
      the hall, so "poor Urba" lands.
- [x] Ulfir sees the pale woman on the stairs; the soldiers prank him with the Haesling
      story, then tell the truth: Huldus seeking refuge, and Ramdal hangs if it's known.
- [ ] **Vidrik tells a story about a huldu kid he saw once.** Vidrik does not appear
      anywhere in the draft yet — he needs an introduction in this scene.
- [ ] **Ulfir hears stories about big battles in the east.** Currently only "skirmishes
      with trolls" is mentioned in passing; the east is where Baldur goes in Chapter 5
      and where the gnome hold sits in Chapter 10, so seed it here.
- [x] Thunder; nobody is manning the wall; "Always Urba."

### Chapter 2 — The Passage
- [x] Ulfir wakes before dawn, walks the battlements alone.
- [x] He finds Urba asleep and covers him with his own fur.

- [x] The pale woman appears; "This is where they used to trade in ancient times."
- [x] The clinking behind the wall — chains, or small pickaxes.
- [x] She flees the first sunlight.

### Chapter 3 — The Journey Back

- [x] The old forest: mist, stench, nine hunched figures crossing the path.
- [x] They got past the fortress somehow — climbed the mountain?

## Beyond Act 1

- [ ] **The ending question:** Lily returns, but she has had a Bloodguard child and can
      have no more. Does Ulfir keep her or not? This is the note the story is aimed at —
      everything above should be laid so that the choice costs him something.

- The plague the Huldus supposedly didn't catch.
- Why the soldiers' eyes glow too bright in the firelight.
- Ulfir's promise to the All-Father: "he would bring goddess to this world."
- The 5000 years of gnomen records in the deep.
