# Characters

Only what the draft actually establishes, plus the questions each character raises.
Keep this file honest — if a detail isn't on the page yet, mark it `[planned]`.

## Ulfir — protagonist
Youngest of the family, from Hrafvik. First trip this far north. Small enough to walk
through a gnome door without much trouble. Missing his two front teeth from an earlier
Bloodguard encounter, and robbed of his market coins by them while his elders watched and
went inside — the shame of that is his engine.
Carries his father's worn crossbow from Chapter 5 on. Loses it to the Bloodguards in
Chapter 7 and gets it back; the gnomes take his bolts but leave him the weapon.
In Chapter 7 he prays to the All-Father and promises, if spared, to *bring goddess to
this world* — the debt the rest of the story presumably collects on.

## Baldur — Ulfir's older brother
Experienced enough to have made the Wolfspine run before. Carries a sword, keeps his hand
on the hilt. Dry, a little smug ("The Bloodguard gave us their blessing"). Doesn't hear or
notice what Thorild does. Leaves for the war in Chapter 5 and gives Ulfir the crossbow.
[planned] May reappear in Chapter 12 at Wolfspine.

## Thorild — cousin, Haldor's son
Black hair, green eyes. The diplomat of the three: it's his father's supplies, his
embrace with Ramdal, his job to say no. Hears things the others don't — the bushes, the
figures in the mist. Possibly just attentive; possibly not.

## Haldor — Thorild's father, chief of Hrafvik
Sends the supplies north. Promises men at dawn and patrols against the Grukks.

## General Ramdal — commander of Wolfspine Fortress
Barrel chest, blacksmith's shoulders, wild black-and-grey beard, black bearskin over
heavy chainmail, grey eyes. Fights with axe and heavy round shield. Holds the empire's
edge with a half-empty keep and no help from the other tribes. Shelters Huldus, which
would hang him if the Bloodguard learned of it. Recognises the Bloodguard mark on the
crossbows instantly and is worried by it.

## Urba — the strange one on the wall
Pale, snow resting on his skin without melting, nose and ears too big, a face "as if his
body was older than his soul." Always the one left manning the wall; the soldiers laugh
about it with a note of melancholy. Ulfir covers him with his own fur while he sleeps.
Never spoken to on the page yet. The outline calls him *the strange troll man*.

## The pale woman — Huldu at Wolfspine
Beautiful, moves with deliberate grace, skin almost translucent. Appears silently, out of
the air. Knows the fortress's ancient parts and the history of the sealed passage. Flees
the first sunlight. The soldiers first joke that she's a Haesling, then say plainly that
she and her kind are Huldus in refuge.
[planned] Reappears at the gnomen court in Chapter 10 to name Ulfir as the one of the
prophecy — so she is not confined to the fortress, and she has business with the gnomes.

## Vidrik — soldier at Wolfspine
[planned] Tells a story about a huldu kid he saw once. Not yet written into any scene.

## Culprit — the young gnome
Unnamed through Chapters 7–9, called Culprit from Chapter 10. Small, comically quick when
he moves, painfully polite even under a boot ("Ouch, that is not nice!"). Keeps a journal
in a beautiful script Ulfir can't read; wears gnomen goggles to write. Apprentice to the
old gnome who dies.

## Fignal / Figdal / Gimdal — the mentor gnome
White beard, looks older than the world. Killed by "Redcloaks." Carried by Ulfir from the
forest to the Vibkriseer's house and then east to the gnome hold for a proper funeral.
**His name is inconsistent in the draft — see `notes/open-questions.md`.**

## The Vibkriseer — healer in the deep forest
A woman in black silk, living in a house with an ancient tree growing through it, past
the reek. Mixes potions, curses while she works, is said to have brought back a stillborn
baby once. One is not supposed to disturb a Vibkriseer. Lily's mother.

## Lily — the Vibkriseer's daughter
Ulfir's age. Black eyes, black silk, black messy hair. Quiet, watchful, possibly reads
minds ("Exactly my thought"). Nurses Ulfir through Chapter 9.
[planned] Returns at the end of the story with a Bloodguard child and unable to have
more — the final choice.

## Thaldrin — King of the Huldus
[planned] "I am Thaldrin, he said." Nothing else yet.

## Groups
- **The Bloodguard / Redcloaks** — the occupying power. Mark: a red claw with a drop of
  blood. Tax Hrafvik, rob Norinheimers openly, hang Huldus and those who shelter them.
  The gnomes call them Redcloaks.
- **The Grukks** — the enemy beyond the wall. One lies dead in Hrafvik's dirt: dark hide.
  Nobody in the trio has seen a living one up close.
- **The Huldus** — refugees, pale, night-going. Trade partners of the gnomes, historically.
  Something about a plague they supposedly escaped.
- **The Haeslings** — the soldiers' bogey story: beautiful after dark, horror beneath,
  skin burns like ash in sunlight. Presented as a joke — but the pale woman *does* flee
  the dawn.
- **The gnomes** — eastern mountain hold, guards with "gnomen boomsticks," a court, and
  5000 years of records kept in the deep.
