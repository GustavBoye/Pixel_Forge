# Morals

A bank for step 2. Take one, then find a base whose value flips when you move it
(see `METHOD.md`). Never write the line into the story.

**The test:** could someone reasonably argue the opposite? Would you hesitate to say it
out loud at a table? If no to either, it's a greeting card — push it further.

⛓ = already has a home in the world's lore.

---

## Belonging and difference

- Finding where you belong can mean making someone else the stranger.
- You can be accepted completely and still be alone, if what they accepted was a version
  of you.
- People don't hate what's different. They hate being reminded that "normal" is a local
  rule.
- The ones who take you in will expect you to help them keep the next ones out.
- Being spared is not the same as being innocent, and nobody who watched you survive will
  believe the difference. ⛓ *(the Huldus and the plague)*

## Power and who holds it

- People will forgive a leader anything except being ordinary.
- Whoever saves you owns a piece of you, and you both know it.
- A man given power to fix one thing will keep it long after the thing is fixed.
- Authority lasts exactly as long as it is useful; kinship buys nothing once it isn't. ⛓
  *(the coup at the gnome mountain)*
- The people who ask to be led will punish you for leading.

## Truth and lies

- A lie that feeds people gets judged by the hungry, not by the honest. ⛓ *(Berrick)*
- Confession is usually for the comfort of the one confessing.
- People don't want the truth. They want to have been right.
- The truth is paid for by whoever hears it, not by whoever tells it.
- Every people is founded on something they have agreed to forget. ⛓ *(the gnome archive)*

## Survival and its cost

- Anyone will drink what they know is poison rather than admit they are lost. ⛓ *(the reek)*
- What you did to survive one winter becomes what your children think people are.
- Mercy is cheap when you're fed.
- The cost of a thing is never paid by the person who chose it.
- You can do everything right and still lose, and the world will call it a lesson.

## Kindness and cruelty

- The kindness you don't remember giving is the one someone else built their life on.
  ⛓ *(Ulfir's fur over the sleeping Urba)*
- Cruelty is rarely personal. That is what makes it unbearable.
- Watching and doing nothing is a choice, and everyone who makes it believes it wasn't.
  ⛓ *(the elders who went inside)*
- Most people are decent right up until it costs them something.
- It's easier to be kind to a stranger than to the person you owe.

## Memory and inheritance

- You inherit your parents' answers to problems that no longer exist.
- What gets remembered isn't what happened — it's what was worth repeating.
- The dead are easier to love than the living, and we use that.
- Children pay for their parents' one good decision as surely as for the bad ones.

## Wanting things

- Wanting more than your parents wanted is treated as an insult to them.
- You get exactly what you asked for, at the size you asked for it. That's the problem.
- The dream survives being wrong about it. ⛓ *(the light was only a rock, and it still
  worked)*
- Fear of a thing outlives the thing.
- The monster stories are about the neighbours.

---

## Turning one into a story

A moral is not a story — it's a verdict. To get a story:

1. Find a **base**: a trait, an object, a difference someone carries.
2. Move it somewhere its value flips.
3. Ask who paid for the flip. That's your step 3.
